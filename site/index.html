What Ferret adds

In calls

Push to talk
Hold a key or a side mouse button to talk, even while you're in a game.
Mic sensitivity
A noise gate keeps keyboard clicks and fans out of the call.
Mute and deafen shortcuts
Work anywhere on your PC, not just in Ferret.
Compact call window
A small card on top of other apps shows who's talking. Turn it off if you'd rather not.
Reconnects on its own
After sleep, a Wi-Fi blip or a dropped call, Ferret gets you back in.

Messages and calls

Reply from notifications
New messages pop up in the corner with a reply box.
Ringtones
Calls ring like a phone, with Ferret-made tones or your own file.
Sounds for specific people
Give your best friend their own ringtone so you know without looking.
Favourite GIFs
Star the ones you keep sending.

Make it yours

Themes
Midnight, Cream and High contrast, in five accent colours.
App icons
Ten of them, including albino and silver ferrets.
Every sound
Swap any notification or call sound for one of ours or your own.

The soundboard

Everyone in the call hears these. Try them.

Or upload your own sounds in the app.

Is it safe?

Do I need a new account?
No. You log in with your normal Stoat account and talk to the same people on the same servers.
Can other people tell I'm using Ferret?
Only if you tell them. Ferret talks to Stoat the same way the official app does. It doesn't automate your account, scrape anything or act as a bot.
Is it official?
No. Ferret is an unofficial mod of Stoat, not affiliated with or endorsed by Stoat / Revolt Platforms Ltd. If something breaks, report it to Ferret, not to Stoat.
Can I check what it does?
Yes. All the code is on GitHub under the AGPL-3.0 licence, with a list of every change made to Stoat's app.
Windows says it doesn't recognise the app
Ferret isn't code-signed yet, which costs money every year. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Only download Ferret from this page or GitHub.
What about Mac?
Not yet. Windows and Linux for now.
