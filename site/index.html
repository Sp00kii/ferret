Stoat, with a few more tricks.

Ferret is a free desktop app for Stoat that stays connected: friends show online, calls rejoin after a drop, and it reconnects after sleep. Same servers, same account, plus push to talk, a soundboard and ringtones.

Free and open source. Updates itself on Windows.

Fixes the things that bug Stoat users most

Your friends show online again
Stoat can leave people stuck on "offline" until you press F5. Ferret refreshes who's online by itself.
Reconnects on its own
After sleep, a locked screen or a Wi-Fi blip, Ferret gets back in straight away instead of hanging.
Calls survive drops
If a call drops, Ferret rejoins it, follows your headset when you unplug it, and you can hear people who joined after you.
Notifications that show up
Desktop notifications, a taskbar unread badge and ringing calls that actually reach you.

What Ferret adds

In calls

Push to talk
Hold a key or a side mouse button to talk, even while you're in a game.
Mic sensitivity
A noise gate keeps keyboard clicks and fans out of the call.
Mute and deafen shortcuts
Work anywhere on your PC, not just in Ferret.
Compact call window
A small card on top of other apps shows who's talking. Turn it off if you'd rather not.
Screen share sound without the echo
Share only the game's sound, or everything except the call, so nobody hears themselves back. Windows 11.
Reconnects on its own
After sleep, a Wi-Fi blip or a dropped call, Ferret gets you back in.

Messages and calls

Reply from notifications
New messages pop up in the corner with a reply box.
Ringtones
Calls ring like a phone, with Ferret-made tones or your own file.
Sounds for specific people
Give your best friend their own ringtone so you know without looking.
Favourite GIFs
Star the ones you keep sending.
Previews for every link
Links that Stoat can't preview still get a card with the title and picture.
Times in everyone's time zone
Pick a date and time; everyone sees it in their own time zone.
Personas
Post with another name and picture for role-play, where the server allows it.
Friend links
Share a link that opens "Add friend" with your name filled in.

Themes

The soundboard

Everyone in the call hears these. Try them.

Or upload your own sounds in the app. Too loud? Turn anyone's soundboard down or off, just for you.

Is it safe?

Do I need a new account?
No. You log in with your normal Stoat account and talk to the same people on the same servers.
Can other people tell I'm using Ferret?
Only if you tell them, or turn on Ferret's Discord status. Ferret talks to Stoat the same way the official app does. It doesn't automate your account, scrape anything or act as a bot.
Is it official?
No. Ferret is an unofficial mod of Stoat, not affiliated with or endorsed by Stoat / Revolt Platforms Ltd. If something breaks, report it to Ferret, not to Stoat.
Can I check what it does?
Yes. All the code is on GitHub under the AGPL-3.0 licence, with a list of every change made to Stoat's app.
Windows says it doesn't recognise the app
Ferret isn't code-signed yet, which costs money every year. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Only download Ferret from this page or GitHub.
What about Mac?
Not yet. Windows and Linux for now.
